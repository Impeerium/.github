## A world worth making your own

We're an Estonian Minecraft community with a love for building, competition and the details that make a server feel like home. This is the development home of Impeerium: the custom gameplay, plugins and creative work behind the worlds we share.

| Build your story | Make your mark |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Survival** · Gather, explore and turn a patch of land into something you're proud of. | **Factions** · Build your base, form alliances and fight for territory with your team. |

Season news, opening dates and updates live on [Discord](https://discord.impeerium.net/).

## Built for our community

- **Gameplay with character.** Custom systems, progression and events shaped around Impeerium.
- **Details that belong together.** Menus, cosmetics and resource-pack artwork with a shared identity.
- **Connected experiences.** Plugins, web tools and Discord integrations that support the community.

Most of our development happens in private repositories. This profile is a window into what we build; community news and player support live on [Discord](https://discord.impeerium.net/).
